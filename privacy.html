Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or precise location for profiling. Non-personal technical data may be processed by your browser and by standard web infrastructure (for example server logs on the host you use to reach the site).

2. Cookies & advertising

We use Google AdSense to help fund hosting. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad delivery and, where allowed, to personalize ads based on this site and others you visit. You can control cookies through your browser settings and industry opt-out tools offered by ad networks.

3. Third-party services

AdSense and other embedded scripts operate under their own policies. When you use our browser tools, most processing happens locally; a small number of utilities may call external APIs when necessary for the feature (for example a public IP lookup). Those panels describe the behavior on the tool page.

4. Data security

We take reasonable care with configuration and updates, but no web transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Do not paste highly sensitive secrets into any online form unless you understand the risk.

5. Contact

If you publish a contact email or form on your deployment, use that channel for questions about this policy.
