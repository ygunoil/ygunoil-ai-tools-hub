Roundup

AI PPT generator without login

Truth-first: most cloud AI deck products ask for an account to save work and prevent abuse. Below we compare lowest-friction options and what you get before sign-in.

Gamma

Gamma — card-based "presentation as webpage" with fast AI layout.

Core features

  • Prompt-to-deck generation
  • Editable cards and themes
  • Embeds and analytics on paid tiers
  • Export options (check current plan)
  • Collaboration in workspace

Pros

  • Fastest polished first draft
  • Modern aesthetic out of the box

Cons

  • Account typically required to persist decks
  • Not classic .pptx-first workflow for everyone

Pricing

Free: limited AI credits on free workspace.
Plus: subscription for exports and branding removal — gamma.app.

Best for

Startup pitch decks, class presentations, and landing-page style decks.

Official site

Beautiful.ai

Beautiful.ai — rule-based smart slides that snap to a design system.

Core features

  • Smart templates and auto-layout
  • Team libraries
  • AI text and image assists (product evolves)
  • Brand controls on higher tiers
  • PowerPoint export on paid plans

Pros

  • Consistent corporate look
  • Less manual alignment than classic PPT

Cons

  • Free trial then paywall for exports
  • Creative layouts less flexible than manual PowerPoint

Pricing

Pro / Team: subscription — verify at beautiful.ai.

Best for

Consulting decks and sales narratives where brand consistency matters.

Official site

Google Slides + Gemini

If you already use Google Workspace, this is often the closest to "I have slides open and want AI now" — account exists for other reasons.

Core features

  • Native .pptx-compatible workflow via Google
  • Gemini side panel in Workspace (availability varies)
  • Real-time collaboration
  • Version history
  • Add-ons ecosystem

Pros

  • No separate vendor for many teams
  • Strong collaboration

Cons

  • Requires Google account — not anonymous
  • AI feature rollout is region and plan dependent

Pricing

Free Google account: Slides free with quotas.
Workspace / Gemini: paid add-ons — Google Slides.

Best for

Schools and companies standardized on Google.

Official site

ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini (for slide copy)

Use ChatGPT for speaker notes and slide-by-slide JSON outlines, Claude when you paste long briefs or RFPs, and Gemini when your outline already lives in Docs — then paste into Gamma or Slides.
