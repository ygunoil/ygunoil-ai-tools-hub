Roundup

AI logo maker free unlimited

Search ads promise "unlimited" logos, but vector masters, font licenses, and trademark clearance are almost always gated. Here is a practical split: ideation free, export paid.

Looka

Looka — brand kit generator with AI logo concepts and mockups.

Core features

  • Prompt-driven logo directions
  • Business card and social mockups
  • Vector and PNG bundles on purchase
  • Font pairing suggestions
  • Brand guidelines PDF (tiered)

Pros

  • Strong first-pass brand board
  • Good for solo founders

Cons

  • High-res export is paid
  • Similar marks may exist — do a trademark search

Pricing

Free: browse concepts.
Brand kit purchase: one-time or subscription — looka.com.

Best for

MVPs that need logo + social headers in one checkout.

Official site

Canva

Canva — Magic Design logo flows plus infinite canvas iterations; export rules depend on assets used.

Core features

  • Magic Design brand templates
  • Massive stock library (Pro-licensed elements)
  • SVG / PNG export
  • Team brand kits
  • Print shop integrations

Pros

  • Closest to unlimited iteration for ideation
  • All-in-one social sizes

Cons

  • Pro elements require Pro subscription
  • True vector simplicity varies by design complexity

Pricing

Free: large template set with free assets only.
Canva Pro: monthly / annual — canva.com.

Best for

Creators who will ship dozens of channel art sizes from one project.

Official site

Shopify Hatchful

Hatchful — simple industry presets with free PNG packs (historically generous for basic logos).

Core features

  • Industry-specific icon sets
  • Social profile crops export together
  • Minimal learning curve
  • No heavy AI prompt UI — template driven
  • Good for first Shopify store

Pros

  • Very fast baseline marks
  • Free PNG bundles for many use cases

Cons

  • Less unique than custom illustrator work
  • Advanced vector editing limited

Pricing

Free: basic logo packs.
Paid upgrades: if Shopify changes packaging — verify in-app — Shopify logo maker.

Best for

First-time merchants who need acceptable marks overnight.

Official site

ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini (for naming + briefs)

Before clicking generate, ask ChatGPT or Claude for naming constraints (avoid generic tech words), then feed the winning brief into Looka or Canva. Gemini can sanity-check translations if you go global.
