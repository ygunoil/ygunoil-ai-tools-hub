Roundup

Best AI essay writing tools free

Honest picks for students and freelancers who need a usable free tier before paying — with clear limits on plagiarism, citations, and school policies.

Always follow your institution's AI policy. These tools assist drafting and editing; they are not a substitute for original research and citation.

Grammarly

Grammarly — clarity, grammar, and tone for essays and emails without replacing your ideas.

Core features

  • Real-time grammar and spelling
  • Tone and conciseness suggestions
  • Plagiarism checker on paid plans (not full free)
  • Browser extension + editor
  • Generative AI rewrite snippets (tiered)

Pros

  • Strong free core for proofreading
  • Ubiquitous in academia and business
  • Low friction onboarding

Cons

  • Advanced AI and plagiarism checks paywalled
  • Can flatten voice if you accept every edit

Pricing

Free: core writing assistance.
Premium / Pro: monthly or annual subscription — verify on grammarly.com.

Best for

Final-pass editing, ESL writers, and scholarship essays where mechanics matter.

Official site

QuillBot

QuillBot — paraphrase, summarizer, and citation helpers aimed at research writing.

Core features

  • Paraphrasing modes (formal, academic, etc.)
  • Summarizer for long PDFs (limits on free)
  • Grammar checker
  • Citation generator
  • Chrome extension

Pros

  • Useful free paraphrase cap for short sections
  • Academic mode is a real differentiator

Cons

  • Heavy paraphrase use can trigger similarity checks
  • Word limits push upgrades quickly

Pricing

Free: limited words per rewrite.
Premium: annual discount common — check quillbot.com.

Best for

Condensing sources, tightening paragraphs, and bilingual students polishing phrasing.

Official site

Jasper

Jasper — marketing-first long form; included because teams often ask for an "essay" workflow for reports and thought leadership.

Core features

  • Brand voice and campaign templates
  • Long blog and one-shot document flows
  • Team workspaces
  • SEO-oriented outlines
  • Integrations (CMS, etc.)

Pros

  • Strong for commercial long-form, not only essays
  • Mature template library

Cons

  • No meaningful "free essay" tier — trial only
  • Pricey for students

Pricing

Free: limited trial if offered.
Paid: seat-based subscription — see jasper.ai.

Best for

Agencies and founders repurposing research into articles — not typical homework.

Official site

ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini (for essays)

ChatGPT is the default for outlining and counter-arguments. Claude often feels better at long PDF context and careful hedging. Gemini shines when you already live in Google Docs and want inline help. Pair any of them with Grammarly or QuillBot for line-level cleanup — see our alternatives roundup.
