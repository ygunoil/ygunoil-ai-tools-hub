Roundup

AI video generator for TikTok

TikTok rewards velocity, native captions, and clear hooks. These three stacks cover mobile-first editing, generative shots, and text-to-video experiments — always export 9:16 and respect music licensing.

CapCut

CapCut — TikTok-adjacent editor with AI captions, remove background, and templates.

Core features

  • Auto captions and translation assists
  • Beat-synced templates
  • AI background removal
  • Mobile and desktop apps
  • Commercial music library (check license per track)

Pros

  • Fastest path from clip to TikTok-ready
  • Strong free feature set for creators

Cons

  • Brand safety and region policy changes
  • Cloud processing — read privacy terms

Pricing

Free: broad creator tier.
CapCut Pro / Cloud: subscription for some exports and storage — capcut.com.

Best for

Daily short-form creators who need captions and templates more than raw generative pixels.

Official site

Runway

Runway — Gen-style video models and editing inside a pro timeline.

Core features

  • Text / image to video generations
  • Green screen and inpainting-style tools
  • Collaboration for teams
  • API for product builders
  • Credit-based usage

Pros

  • Cutting-edge generative quality tier
  • Serious for ads and music videos

Cons

  • Credits exhaust quickly on long clips
  • Steeper learning curve than CapCut

Pricing

Free: limited trial credits.
Standard / Pro / Unlimited: tiers — runwayml.com.

Best for

B-roll generation, surreal transitions, and branded spots where you composite in After Effects or CapCut after.

Official site

Pika

Pika — playful text-to-video tuned for social experiments and meme formats.

Core features

  • Short clip generation from prompts
  • Lip-sync and effect experiments (check ToS)
  • Community feeds
  • Discord / web workflows (product changes)
  • Credit packs

Pros

  • Great for viral visual gags
  • Rapid iteration for hooks

Cons

  • Less predictable for strict brand guidelines
  • Face / likeness policies — read rules

Pricing

Free: limited credits when offered.
Paid: subscription or packs — pika.art.

Best for

Creators testing surreal hooks before polishing audio in CapCut.

Official site

ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini (for TikTok scripts)

Use ChatGPT for punchy hooks and comment-bait questions, Claude for longer storytelling beats, and Gemini when you want YouTube + Search grounding for fact-heavy niches — then cut in CapCut.
